About Us

We are the Mercedes-AMG PETRONAS Formula One Team. Based in Brackley and Brixworth, we are a team of passionate racers and innovators, driven by the relentless pursuit of performance.

  • 8x Consecutive Constructors' World Champions.
  • Over 100+ Grand Prix Victories in the modern era.
  • Pioneers of cutting-edge hybrid powertrain technology.

Our legacy is built on the racetrack, but our vision extends beyond it. Led by Toto Wolff, our team combines the precision of German engineering with the fighting spirit of motorsport royalty. We race not just to win on Sunday, but to push the boundaries of automotive technology for tomorrow. Through triumph and adversity, the Silver Arrows remain a symbol of excellence.
